# Motoluxe UI Showcase

Build a frontend-only e-commerce UI for "Motoluxe" — a premium autocare & bike care products brand. No backend, no auth, no payments — pure UI/UX with React + Tailwind.

Theme: Bold black & red brand palette, with yellow used as an accent/highlight (badges, CTAs, "premium" tags). Industrial, high-performance, automotive aesthetic — sharp diagonal cuts, bold condensed typography, glossy card surfaces. Should feel premium and aggressive, not soft or minimal-boring.

Pages:

Home — Full-width hero banner (product in action, e.g. chain/engine close-up) with bold tagline + CTA button. Below: a "Shop by Category" section with 3 large category cards (Chain Care, Engine Care, Body & Detailing — or similar autocare groupings), each with hover zoom/glow effect. Then a featured products horizontal scroll row. Then a "Why Motoluxe" trust strip (ISO badge, German formulation, feedback contact) with icons. Footer with logo, links, contact info.

Category pages (3) — Each opens to a product grid of sub-products under that category (e.g. Chain Care → Chain Cleaner, Chain Lube, Degreaser). Product cards show image, name, short tagline, price placeholder, "View Details" hover state.

Product Detail page — Image gallery, product name, key benefits (checklist style like the label), application/usage steps, price, "Add to Cart" (UI only).

Contact Us page — Simple form (name, email, message) + brand contact details, styled to match theme.
